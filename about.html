About Qazaq Trails

Inspired by Kazakhstan. Created for travelers.

OUR STORY

Discover the Beauty of Our Homeland

Qazaq Trails is an interactive travel guide created by three students of Astana IT University. Our project helps people discover the beautiful landscapes, historical places and cultural heritage of Kazakhstan.

Kazakhstan is a country of endless steppes, majestic mountains, crystal-clear lakes and modern cities. We believe that traveling is one of the best ways to explore its unique identity.

Our website combines tourism and technology to make travel planning easier, more accessible and enjoyable for everyone.
